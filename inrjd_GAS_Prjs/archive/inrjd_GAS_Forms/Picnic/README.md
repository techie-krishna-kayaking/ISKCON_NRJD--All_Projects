# Spiritual Picnic Registration — Archive

An older Apps Script registration form for a spiritual picnic. `Code.gs` serves `Form.html` and writes submitted participant and contact details to a configured Google Sheet.

This copy is retained for reference and is not confirmed as the active registration system. Before reuse, verify the target spreadsheet and tab, column order, consent fields, privacy/access settings, and event dates. The form collects personal and emergency-contact information; restrict access to the sheet and do not share real submissions publicly.
